## 2. Literature Review & Technical Justification

### 2.1 The Evolution of Resume Information Extraction (RIE)
Traditional Resume Information Extraction (RIE) relied primarily on:
1. **Rule-Based Parsers**: Brittle regular expressions and keyword dictionaries that fail when encountering non-standard section headers, multi-column layouts, or irregular spacing.
2. **Classical Named Entity Recognition (NER)**: Conditional Random Fields (CRFs) or SpaCy statistical models trained on small domain-specific corpora. These models frequently fail on ambiguous titles and produce fragmented job summaries.
3. **Heavy Deep Learning OCR (e.g. LayoutLM, Docling, PyTorch)**: While layout-aware, these frameworks require multi-gigabyte GPU dependencies (>4 GB), CUDA drivers, and suffer from high cold-start times (>30 seconds), rendering them impractical for lightweight desktop deployment on recruiter laptops.

### 2.2 The ResumeDrop Hybrid Architecture Advantage
ResumeDrop solves the RIE trilemma (accuracy vs. footprint vs. throughput) through an evidence-based **Hybrid Architecture**:
- **Layer 1 (Native Ingestion)**: Uses `PyMuPDF` (`fitz`) and `python-docx` for layout-preserving text extraction without deep learning dependencies (~250 MB total environment footprint vs. >4 GB).
- **Layer 2 (Deterministic Gatekeeper)**: Regular expressions handle high-entropy, low-ambiguity scalar fields (RFC 5322 email patterns, E.164 and Indian 10-digit mobile phone numbers, verified LinkedIn/GitHub/Portfolio URLs).
- **Layer 3 (Generative LLM Intelligence)**: Multi-key pooled NVIDIA NIM inference (`meta/llama-3.3-70b-instruct`) parses complex career trajectories, multi-degree hierarchies, bullet-point responsibilities, and project technical stacks into a strict JSON schema with shared rate limiting (<=40 RPM).
- **Layer 4 (Post-Extraction Validation & Shielding)**: Canonical Pydantic v2 validation enforces temporal consistency (graduation year bounds 1950–2035) and strips formula injection vectors (`=`, `+`, `-`, `@`) prior to writing openpyxl spreadsheet rows.
